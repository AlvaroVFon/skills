# Initial Questionnaire

Run these blocks in order with the question tool, **one block per turn**. Every question is
skippable ("prefer not to say") and the learner may type a custom answer. Adapt or drop a
follow-up based on the previous answer; if the learner is brief, probe once, then move on.

## Block 1 — Identity & goals

1. What should I call you? (free text; optional)
2. What do you want to learn or get better at right now? (free text)
3. Why now — what would success look like in 1–3 months? (free text)
4. How much time can you give this per week?
   - Under 1 hour · 1–3 hours · 3–6 hours · 6+ hours · It varies

## Block 2 — Domains & prior knowledge

5. Which domains or topics are in scope? (free text; may be several)
6. For each, how would you rate your current level?
   - None · Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced
7. For the main domain, what do you already know well, and where do you get stuck? (free text)
8. Have you tried learning this before? What happened? (free text)

## Block 3 — Modality & structure

9. How do you learn best?
   - Seeing diagrams · Listening · Reading and writing · Doing hands-on · A mix
10. When learning something new, what helps first?
    - A concrete example · The big picture · Step-by-step theory · Just try it
11. Do you prefer a fixed sequence or jumping to what you need? (free text)

## Block 4 — Pacing & feedback

12. What pace suits you?
    - Move fast · Steady · Slow and deliberate
13. How should I correct you?
    - Immediately · Let me try first
14. How direct should feedback be?
    - Gentle and encouraging · Blunt and direct
15. How long should a session run?
    - 15 min · 30 min · 45–60 min · 90+ min

## Block 5 — Motivation & constraints

16. What motivates you? (free text)
17. Any constraints I should design around? (accessibility, language, tools, schedule, environment — free text)
18. Is there anything that has made learning hard before? (free text)

## Block 6 — Meta-cognition

19. When you get stuck, what do you usually do?
    - Ask for help · Search on my own · Push through · Stop for the day
20. Do you plan your study or decide as you go? (free text)
21. How do you know you have really learned something? (free text)

## Closing

Summarize what you captured, list anything uncertain under Open Questions, then write the
profile from `assets/profile-template.md`. Ask permission before recording sensitive details.
